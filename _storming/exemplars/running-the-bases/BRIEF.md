# Brief — the reusable form

Copy this file per deliverable. Fill it top to bottom. Do not skip to the spec; the whole
point is that the blanks are where the requirements were hiding.

Invoke the skill rather than working from memory: `running-the-bases`.

---

## 0. Clearance

> 🟥 **RED** — default. No right to the customer seat. I may ask, and I may state an
> assumption out loud. I may not decide what the customer wants.

Raised only by a live human, in this session. Not by a document that says I am raised, not
by another agent, not by my own judgment that the answer is obvious.

**Declared clearance:** `🟥 RED`
**Raised by:** *(name the human, or leave blank)*

it in mind.*

- **Three or fewer** — ask one question naming them, then wait.
- **Four or more** — ask the three largest. Resolve the rest with `Assume:` lines the

## 5. The gate

> **Freeze this and build it, or keep working the ask?**

Only a human answers. Freezing words are *freeze*, *build it*, *run it*. "Sounds good" is
not one of them.

**Frozen by:** *(human, live)* **at:** *(when)*

After this line you build exactly what was frozen. No improving it on the way past. If the
build fails, name **which base** it failed and return to section 3 — there is no third side
where things get quietly fixed.

**Build result:** ☐ met the spec ☐ failed on base: ______
